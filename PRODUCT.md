# Online book

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript, generated from the existing Markdown with Python. Implementation choice delegated by the author's instruction to proceed with the proposed GitHub Pages site.

## Users
Chinese and English readers of the author's practical business book, opening shared links on phones or reading on computers.

## Product Purpose
Read either edition without downloading a large document, navigate chapters and worksheets, and find the existing PDF and Word editions.

## Capabilities and Constraints
GitHub Pages on the existing public repository. Preserve the approved Chinese and English source text. Keep the eight marked English PNGs byte for byte. No accounts, analytics, backend, or external font dependency. Language switching reaches the corresponding chapter or worksheet.

## Brand Commitments
The book's established green and cream cover identity, title, author and supplied artwork. The reference site was provided as an example of github.io hosting, not a visual reference.

## Evidence on Hand
Chinese chapters, tools and cover under book/. English chapters, worksheets, notes, figures and front matter under book/en/. English public release en-v1.1-20261010; Chinese v3.6 PDF and Word under edition/v3.6/.

## Product Principles
Prioritize legible long-form reading, mobile sharing, direct chapter links and source fidelity. Retain the published English review scope and existing sharing terms.
