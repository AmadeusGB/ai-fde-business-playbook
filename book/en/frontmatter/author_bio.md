# About the author

## Guo Bin

### Bringing AI into real work and making the results stand up to scrutiny

Guo Bin holds a master's degree in computer technology from Beihang University. He has experience in software development, systems engineering, and project delivery, and now focuses on designing and deploying AI systems for enterprises.

His engineering work has led him to place particular importance on responsibility and verification in delivery: starting with real business operations to understand the problem, defining project scope and acceptance criteria, and attending to the system's continued operation and improvement after it goes live.

In *Bringing AI into Business Operations*, he draws on engineering experience and case research to help business owners decide which projects deserve investment, who should deliver them, and how to accept the work and improve it over time. He also helps technical teams turn business objectives into system designs, delivery standards, and arrangements for ongoing operation.
