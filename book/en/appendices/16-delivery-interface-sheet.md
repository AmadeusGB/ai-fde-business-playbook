# Worksheet 16 Check delivery handover responsibilities

Designed by the author. Choose an actual task. In each row, record who is responsible, how completion is confirmed, and what remains unresolved. One person may hold several roles, but each decision must be recorded separately. For unclear matters, specify who investigates and by when.

| Validation checks | Checker: ___; report scope: ___; items not covered: ___ |
| Business handover | Recipient: ___; usable scope: ___; return conditions: ___ |
| Procurement and payment | Contracting entity: ___; acceptance confirmation: ___; payment status / date and evidence: ___; outstanding actions: ___ |
| Operations and maintenance | Intake point: ___; party responsible for resolving issues: ___; takeover record: ___ |
| Feedback from experience | Owner of business rules and evidence: ___; issue sent to whom: ___; next decision: ___ |

Sample one row: who provides the materials, and who confirms readiness to take over? If takeover is not yet possible, who decides the next step and within what period? Do not enter “completed” without sufficient evidence. This sheet does not replace a contract or formal authorization.
