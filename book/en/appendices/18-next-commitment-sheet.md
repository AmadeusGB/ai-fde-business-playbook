# Worksheet 18 Next-stage decision sheet

Purpose: approve the next piece of work for an actual task. Enter names, dates, and evidence indexes first, then obtain confirmation from an authorized person. A blank sheet is not release authorization. Routine actions continue under existing authority; review on change or at the endpoint. Cite existing worksheets for detailed budget, testing, and takeover records rather than copying them here.

Completion check: ask the recipient to explain what they receive tomorrow, what they may not do, and whom to contact if conditions change. If any answer is unclear, complete the decision first. See Table 18-6 for a teaching example; actual names, ceilings, and records cannot be copied from it.
