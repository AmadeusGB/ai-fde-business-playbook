# Worksheet 15 Economic check for one category of delivery service

Designed by the author. Choose one service category with clear scope. For amounts, state currency, period, and whether actual or estimated. For unknown items, state who will check and by when. Do not substitute deployment counts for revenue.

Buyers can first complete charges and service obligations under “Service and revenue,” “Buyer burden,” and “This decision,” then check maintenance personnel, support scope, and replacement arrangements. Leave unavailable provider cost and renewal data unknown; do not infer profit from a quote. Provider operators can complete project, shared investment, and cohort accounts. Customer acquisition costs include unsuccessful presales investment. Allocate gaps between projects, replacement, and cross-border collaboration costs by actual activity, avoiding double counting.

This is not a financial statement; completing the form does not replace evidence of fulfillment and outcomes.
